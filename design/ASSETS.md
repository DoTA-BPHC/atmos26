# Asset log

How every shipped asset was made, so it can be regenerated.

## Hero logo layers — `public/logo/*.webp`, `src/data/logoLayers.json`
- Source: `atmos-website.jpg` (official artwork, fest design team) plus the team's own layer exports in `design/source/` (`letters.png`, `machine_hand.png`, 3000 px, same framing as the logo).
- `scripts/logo_segment.py` — SAM 2.1 (hiera-large, via transformers) cuts object masks from hand-placed prompts into `design/logo/masks/`.
- `scripts/logo_layers.py` — refines masks against a clean plate (drop shadows, wires), fills hidden pixels (vortex/ring by polar interpolation, black outside the ring, letters and machine-hand fingertips from `design/source`, the human fingers behind the M by LaMa), writes lossless WebP layers.
- `scripts/verify_logo.py` — re-stacks the layers and fails unless they match the JPEG exactly (currently mean and max difference 0).
- Needs: Python 3.12 with torch, transformers, opencv, pillow; LaMa weights at `D:/atmos-ai/models/lama/big-lama.pt`.

## The Ascension's machines — `public/world/*.glb`
- Modelled procedurally in Blender 5.2, one module per floor in `scripts/blender/world/` (`clockwork.py`, `steam.py`, `silicon.py`, `genome.py`, `intelligence.py`; shared helpers and materials in `common.py`). Nothing is scanned, bought or generated by an image model.
- `blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/blender/world_chambers.py -- <floor> [--preview] [--no-bake]` builds the floor, bakes ambient occlusion into a vertex colour attribute with Cycles (GPU), optionally renders a Cycles preview to `design/review/world/<floor>.png`, and exports `public/world/<floor>.raw.glb` (gitignored). Materials travel by name; motion hints (gear ratios, crank geometry, trace centrelines, network units) travel as node extras.
- `npm run pack:world` (`scripts/pack_world.mjs`, gltf-transform) welds, lightly simplifies, quantises and meshopt-compresses each raw export into `public/world/<floor>.glb` (500–650 KB each), keeping the node tree and material names the site relies on.
- The clockwork's train uses real ratios (escape 30, third 64/8, centre 60/8, great 96/12); the genome's sequence spells ATMOS'26 in two bits per base.

## Floor stills — `public/world/stills/*.webp`
- Rendered from the live site, not re-drawn: `http://localhost:5173/?tier=high&capture` at 1600×1000 (capture mode keeps each machine centred), with the page chrome hidden, each floor scrolled to its standing moment (clockwork 0.62, steam 0.6, silicon 0.6, genome 0.62, intelligence 0.6 of its segment). Screenshots via Playwright, resized to 1280×800 and saved as WebP (quality 82) in PIL. Used for reduced motion, devices without WebGL, and inner-page headers there.

Shaft-top stills `top.webp` (1280x800, from a 1600x1000 capture) and `top-portrait.webp` (720x1280): same capture mode, the journey scrolled to progress 0 (the hero pose, camera resting above the top collar). They sit behind the hero logo until the live shaft takes over.

## Point clouds — `public/shapes/*.bin`
- `scripts/blender/era_shapes.py` (Blender 5.2, headless: `blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/blender/era_shapes.py`) models hands (from `design/source/rigged_lowpoly_hand.glb`), pocket watch, locomotive, circuit-board city, DNA, brain and concentric rings procedurally, samples 12,000 surface points each (int16 xyz). Reference GLBs land in `design/3d/`. Only `halo.bin` (the logo's ring, from `scripts/halo_points.py`) is used by the Ascension, for the summit.
